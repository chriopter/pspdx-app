The font PSPDX carries (app/assets/font.pgf in its source, compiled into the
program) is the unmodified free replacement ltn8.pgf from PPSSPP v1.20.4.
It is not a font dumped from Sony firmware.

Source: https://github.com/hrydgard/ppsspp/blob/v1.20.4/assets/flash0/font/ltn8.pgf
